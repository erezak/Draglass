# Welcome to Draglass

Draglass is a local-first knowledge base that runs entirely on your device. Every note lives as a plain Markdown file inside a vault folder you choose — no accounts, no cloud, no telemetry.

## The Demo Vault

You are reading the **demo vault** — a built-in, interactive guide that ships with Draglass. It opens automatically the first time you launch the app, and you can return to it anytime via the Command Palette (**Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + P → Open Demo Vault**).

Every note here is a real Markdown file. Browse them in the left sidebar, follow the [[Wikilinks]] between them, edit anything you like, and watch connections appear in the right-hand panels and the [[Graph View]]. The demo vault is not a slide deck — it is a working vault that demonstrates every feature by using it.

## Where to begin

If this is your first time, start with the [[Quick Start Guide]]. It walks through opening a vault, navigating notes, and the keyboard shortcuts that make everything fast.

From there, the notes below explore different parts of the app by putting them to use:

- [[Wikilinks]] — how `[[double brackets]]` connect your thinking

- [[Searching Your Vault]] — the Quick Switcher, Global Search, and how they complement each other
- [[Graph View]] — a visual map of every note and every link in the vault

## The knowledge-base side

The rest of this vault is a small, interconnected web of topic notes — [[Philosophy]], [[Science]], [[History]], [[Technology]], and more. They exist to show what a real vault looks like: ideas linked to ideas, structure emerging from the links themselves.

Open the [[Graph View]] now and you will see how those topics cluster together.

## Opening your own vault

When you are ready to work with your own files, press the vault-folder icon in the top bar to choose any folder on your device. Draglass will remember your last vault on the next launch (you can turn this off in [[Customizing Draglass|Settings]]).

To come back to this demo vault later, open the Command Palette (**Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + P**) and choose **Open Demo Vault**.

Happy writing!
