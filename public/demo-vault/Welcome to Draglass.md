# Welcome to Draglass

Welcome to your knowledge base! Draglass is a local-first, privacy-focused note-taking application that helps you organize your thoughts.

## Getting Started

- [[Quick Start Guide]] - Learn the basics
- [[Creating Notes]] - How to create and manage notes
- [[Wikilinks]] - Connect your ideas with bidirectional links
- [[Organization Strategies]] - Tips for organizing your vault

## Key Features

Draglass offers several powerful features:

- **Local-first**: Your data stays on your device
- **Wikilinks**: Create connections between notes with `[[Note Name]]`
- **Markdown Support**: Write in plain Markdown
- **Graph View**: Visualize connections between your notes
- **Search**: Quickly find what you need
