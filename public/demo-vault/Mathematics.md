# Mathematics

Mathematics is the study of numbers, quantities, shapes, and patterns. It's both a pure science and a tool for understanding the world.

## Major Branches

### Pure Mathematics
- **Number Theory**: Properties of numbers
- **Abstract Algebra**: Structures and operations
- **Analysis**: Limits, continuity, calculus
- **Topology**: Properties of space
- **Logic**: Foundations of mathematical reasoning

### Applied Mathematics
- **Statistics**: Data analysis and probability
- **Optimization**: Finding best solutions
- **Numerical Analysis**: Computational methods
- **Mathematical Modeling**: Representing real systems

## Mathematics in Other Fields

Math underpins many disciplines:

### Science
[[Science]] relies heavily on mathematics:
- [[Physics]] uses calculus and differential equations
- [[Chemistry]] uses algebra and statistics
- [[Biology]] uses statistics and modeling

### Computer Science
[[Computer Science]] is built on math:
- Algorithms use discrete mathematics
- Graphics use linear algebra and geometry
- Machine learning uses statistics and calculus
- Cryptography uses number theory

### Technology
[[Technology]] applies mathematics:
- Engineering calculations
- Signal processing
- Data compression
- [[Innovation]] often starts with math

## Historical Development

Mathematics evolved through [[History]]:
- Ancient counting systems
- [[Ancient Greece]] geometry (Euclid, Pythagoras)
- Arab algebra developments
- [[Enlightenment]] calculus (Newton, Leibniz)
- Modern abstract mathematics
