# Innovation

Innovation is the process of creating new ideas, methods, or products that provide value and drive progress.

## Types of Innovation

### Technological Innovation
New [[Technology]] and tools:
- [[Computer Science]] advances
- Engineering breakthroughs
- Scientific applications from [[Science]]
- Medical innovations

### Process Innovation
New ways of doing things:
- Manufacturing methods
- Business models
- [[Workflow]] improvements
- Organizational structures

### Social Innovation
New approaches to social challenges:
- [[Education]] methods
- Governance models
- Community development
- Environmental solutions

## Innovation Process

Typical stages:
1. **Inspiration**: Observe problems
2. **Ideation**: Generate solutions
3. **Implementation**: Build and test
4. **Iteration**: Refine and improve
5. **Scaling**: Expand impact

## Foundations of Innovation

Innovation builds on:
- [[Science]] - Understanding principles
- [[Mathematics]] - Modeling and analysis
- [[Technology]] - Tools and platforms
- [[Critical Thinking]] - Problem-solving
- [[Philosophy]] - Questioning assumptions
