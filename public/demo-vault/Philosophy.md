# Philosophy

Philosophy is the study of fundamental questions about existence, knowledge, values, reason, mind, and language.

## Major Branches

### Metaphysics
The study of reality and existence. Questions like:
- What is the nature of reality?
- Do we have free will?
- What is consciousness?

Related to [[Science]] in its investigation of nature.

### Epistemology
The study of knowledge:
- What can we know?
- How do we know what we know?
- What is truth?

See [[Scientific Method]] for applied epistemology.

### Ethics
The study of morality and right action. Connects to:
- [[Social Philosophy]]
- [[Political Theory]]
- Personal [[Workflow]] and values

### Logic
The study of valid reasoning, foundational to:
- [[Mathematics]]
- [[Computer Science]]
- [[Critical Thinking]]

## Historical Periods

- [[Ancient Greece]] - Socrates, Plato, Aristotle
- [[Enlightenment]] - Reason and individual rights
- [[Modern Philosophy]] - 20th century developments

## Practical Applications

Philosophy isn't just abstract. It influences:
- [[Ethics]] in [[Technology]]
- [[Scientific Method]] in [[Science]]
- [[Critical Thinking]] in all fields
- Personal [[Workflow]] and decision-making

## Influential Thinkers

Great philosophers have shaped [[History]] and [[Literature]]:
- Their ideas inform [[Political Theory]]
- They influence [[Art and Culture]]
- They shape how we approach [[Education]]
