# Critical Thinking

Critical thinking is the disciplined process of analyzing, evaluating, and synthesizing information to form sound judgments.

## Core Skills

### Analysis
- Break down complex ideas
- Identify assumptions
- Recognize patterns
- See connections (use [[Wikilinks]]!)

### Evaluation
- Assess evidence quality
- Identify bias
- Test arguments
- Compare alternatives

### Synthesis
- Combine ideas from multiple sources
- Create new understanding
- Build frameworks
- Generate insights

## Applications

Critical thinking matters across domains:

### Science
[[Science]] requires critical thinking:
- Evaluate experimental design
- Assess statistical significance
- Review peer research
- Apply [[Scientific Method]]

### Philosophy
[[Philosophy]] develops critical thinking:
- Logical argumentation
- Conceptual analysis
- [[Ethics|Ethical]] reasoning
- Examining assumptions

### Everyday Life
Daily decisions benefit from:
- Evaluating news and information
- Making informed choices
- Solving problems
- Understanding [[Technology]] impacts

## Historical Development

Critical thinking evolved through [[History]]:
- [[Ancient Greece]] - Socratic method
- [[Enlightenment]] - Emphasis on reason
- Modern era - Formal logic and scientific method
- Today - Information literacy in digital age

## Barriers to Critical Thinking

Common obstacles:
- Cognitive biases
- Emotional reasoning
- Group think
- Information overload
- Confirmation bias

## Developing Critical Thinking

Practices to improve:
1. **Question assumptions**: Challenge what seems obvious
2. **Seek evidence**: Don't accept claims without support
3. **Consider alternatives**: Multiple perspectives matter
4. **Reflect**: Use [[Daily Notes]] for metacognition
5. **Connect ideas**: Build knowledge with [[Wikilinks]]
