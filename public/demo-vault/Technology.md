# Technology

Technology is the application of scientific knowledge for practical purposes, shaping how we live and work.

## Definition and Scope

Technology encompasses:
- Tools and machines
- Software and algorithms
- Systems and processes
- Methods and techniques

## Historical Development

Technology has evolved alongside [[History]]:
- Ancient tools and agriculture
- Industrial Revolution
- Information Age
- Current [[Innovation]] in AI and biotech

## Key Domains

### Computer Science
[[Computer Science]] is fundamental to modern technology:
- Programming and algorithms
- Data structures
- Artificial intelligence
- Networks and distributed systems

### Information Technology
- Software development
- Database systems
- Web technologies
- Cloud computing

### Engineering
- Mechanical systems
- Electrical systems
- Civil infrastructure
- Biomedical devices

## Technology and Society

Technology intersects with many areas:

- **[[Ethics]]**: Privacy, AI safety, environmental impact
- **[[Political Theory]]**: Digital governance, surveillance
- **[[Education]]**: Online learning, digital literacy
- **Economy**: Automation, new industries

## The Scientific Foundation

Technology builds on [[Science]]:
- [[Physics]] enables electronics
- [[Chemistry]] creates new materials
- [[Biology]] inspires biomimicry
- [[Mathematics]] underpins algorithms

## Innovation Cycles

[[Innovation]] follows patterns:
1. Scientific discovery
2. Technological application
3. Market adoption
4. Societal impact
5. Reflection and regulation (via [[Ethics]])

## Personal Technology

Technology shapes our [[Workflow]]:
- Knowledge management tools like [[Welcome to Draglass]]
- Communication platforms
- Productivity applications
- Learning resources

## Critical Perspective

As discussed in [[Philosophy]] and [[Critical Thinking]]:
- Technology isn't neutral
- It reflects human values
- It shapes behavior and thought
- It requires ethical consideration

## Future Directions

Emerging technologies:
- Artificial intelligence
- Quantum computing
- Biotechnology
- Clean energy

These will shape future [[History]] and require ongoing [[Ethics]] discussions.
