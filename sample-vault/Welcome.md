# Welcome to your vault

This is a small, portable Markdown workspace. Your notes are ordinary files.

- Open a note in the sidebar.
- Edit it, then **Save** with Cmd/Ctrl + S.
- Choose **Read** to follow [[Connected thinking|a connection]].
- Search for a word in any note.

## Your first connection

A note can point to another note with `[[Note name]]`.

Read [[Ideas]] for a place to start.
