01 / WRITING

Stay in the document.

Markdown underneath. A readable document in front of you.

Write in Live view

Live editing renders formatting while keeping your text editable. Switch to reading view when you want to focus on the result, or source mode when you need the Markdown itself.

A SMALL EXAMPLE

Field notes

The observation platform brings together people, systems, and the decisions behind them.

  • ✓ Capture the observation
  • ✓ Connect it to the right system
  • Sketch the next experiment
Clarity comes from seeing how the pieces fit.

Save as you go

Edits save automatically after a short pause. Draglass watches for external changes, so your desktop workspace can stay in step with other tools. The last completed save or load wins.

Tables that remain Markdown

Edit a table cell in place. Click a link’s text to follow it, or click the blank space around the link to edit its cell. Escaped aliases keep their targets intact.

Connect without breaking your flow

Use wiki links, aliases, backlinks, and note, heading, or block embeds. Images, PDFs, audio, video, and other attachments stay close to the writing.

Bring the right notes into the room

Start a new conversation even when no note is open. Mention an existing note to include its metadata and contents in the conversation.

Turn a conversation into a change

AI can propose note creation and edits. Review the proposed changes before applying them, with an undo path. Keep separate conversations for different topics.

Local files, explicit context

Your vault stays on your filesystem. When you use AI, the selected provider receives the context needed for that request, including notes you mention. Store provider credentials in Settings.

AI is optional. Writing, organizing, search, diagrams, and graph exploration work with local files.

05 / ORGANIZATION & NAVIGATION

Make a little room.

Find your way back. Keep the links intact. Set the workspace up for the way you think.

Find what matters

Search note contents and properties, browse tags, or jump to a note with the quick switcher. Explore backlinks and the local graph when the next step is a connection away.

Move the note, keep its context

Rename and move files or folders with link updates. Restore deleted files from vault trash. Configure attachment locations and note organization in Settings.

A workspace that makes room

Collapse the explorer or AI sidebar when you want to focus. Choose light or dark mode, adjust editor preferences, and use the command palette to reach your next action.

Command palette
⌘ / Ctrl + Shift + P

Open just the file

Supported Markdown and drawing files can open directly in Draglass. Files outside your current vault open in a clearly identified detached mode. Detached Markdown starts in Live view.

Structure without ceremony

Create ontology-aware notes from configurable entity templates. Manage bundled features through Settings, with categories beside their controls.

A FEW PRACTICAL THINGS

Before you move in.

Do I need AI?

No. Writing, organizing, linking, diagrams, search, and graph exploration work with local files. Configure an AI connection when you want it.

What happens to my files?

Notes stay as Markdown in your chosen folder. Draglass saves edits automatically and watches for external changes. Keep backups or version control as you would with other important files.

Can I open just one file?

Yes. Files outside your current vault can open in detached mode. Detached Markdown files open in Live view, with a clear indicator that they’re outside the vault.

Can I try example knowledge?

Yes. The bundled demo vault is editable and includes connected notes, diagrams, attachments, and guided tours.

Can I edit the notes on this website?

No. This is a read-only product tour presented as a Markdown workspace. Download the desktop app to create and edit your own notes.

Does the website send anything to AI?

No. The examples here are static illustrations. AI requests happen in the desktop app when you use a configured connection.
