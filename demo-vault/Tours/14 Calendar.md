# Calendar: notes, writing and optional tasks

Run **Open Calendar** or select the calendar icon on the right. The compact calendar appears immediately in the right pane; the editor and its tabs stay in place. Resize the right pane to watch the grid follow its width. Calendar options are below the grid. Set Month to 2026-11, then select November 1 to open [[Journal/2026-11-01]]. Hover its grey writing dots for the exact count. A red dot marks a day with matching tasks due. Clicking a day with one dated note opens it directly. A day without notes or matching tasks offers a dated New note dialog; Cancel leaves the vault untouched. The dots represent current dated-note content, not reconstructed writing history.

In Settings → Calendar, turn Show writing statistics off/on and change First day of week from Monday to Sunday or any other weekday. The grid alignment changes without modifying a note.

In Settings → Tasks, toggle Show tasks on Calendar. With it off, the date grid and dated notes remain. Disable Tasks under Core plugins: Calendar should still work. Re-enable Tasks and its integration to inspect station deadlines, date bases and normal task Undo.

Calendar itself is a core plugin. Disable it to hide its right-side entry and command, then re-enable it. Restart with Calendar open to check that its selected month returns.
