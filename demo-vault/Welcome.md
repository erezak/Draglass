# Welcome to Draglass

Welcome to **Lumen field station**, a fictional coastal research project. Explore the people, goals, systems, decisions and original evidence that hold this little organization together. All examples and identities are fictional; all attachments are bundled locally.

This is an editable vault. Try things, follow links and change text. Your changes save automatically. No AI requests are made until you ask for one.
