# Ancient Greece

Ancient Greece was a civilization that flourished from around 800 BCE to 146 BCE, profoundly influencing Western culture.

## Key Contributions

### Philosophy
Ancient Greece birthed [[Philosophy]] as we know it:
- **Socrates**: Socratic method, ethics
- **Plato**: Theory of Forms, The Republic
- **Aristotle**: Logic, natural sciences, ethics

These thinkers shaped [[Critical Thinking]] and ethical inquiry for millennia.

### Democracy
Athens developed early democracy:
- Citizen participation in government
- Public debate and rhetoric
- Foundations for modern [[Political Theory]]

### Literature and Drama
Greek [[Literature]] remains influential:
- Epic poetry: Homer's Iliad and Odyssey
- Tragedy: Sophocles, Euripides, Aeschylus
- Comedy: Aristophanes
- These works still appear in [[Education]]

### Science and Mathematics
Greek contributions to [[Science]]:
- [[Mathematics]]: Euclid's geometry, Pythagoras
- Physics: Early theories of matter
- Astronomy: Geocentric model
- Medicine: Hippocrates

## Historical Context

Ancient Greece in [[History]]:
- City-states (poleis): Athens, Sparta
- Persian Wars
- Peloponnesian War
- Alexander the Great's empire
- Roman conquest

## Cultural Legacy

Greek culture influences:
- Architecture and sculpture
- [[Literature]] — epic, drama, poetry
- [[Philosophy]] — Western philosophical tradition
- [[Science]] — scientific inquiry methods
- [[Political Theory]] — democratic ideals

## Learning from Ancient Greece

Studying Ancient Greece helps us understand:
- Origins of Western thought
- Development of [[Critical Thinking]]
- Foundations of ethical thinking
- Evolution of [[Political Theory]]
- Roots of [[Science]] and [[Mathematics]]

The Greeks asked fundamental questions still relevant in [[Philosophy]], [[Science]], and [[Political Theory]] today.
