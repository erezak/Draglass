# Organization Strategies

How you organize your vault shapes how you think. Draglass gives you plain folders, [[Wikilinks]], [[Backlinks]], and the [[Graph View]] — the rest is up to you. Here are a few proven patterns.

## Folder strategies

### Flat vault
Keep every note at the root. Rely entirely on links to create structure. This works well for smaller vaults and promotes heavy interlinking.

### Topic folders
Group notes by subject — a `Science/` folder, a `Philosophy/` folder, and so on. Draglass lets you create folders from the left-sidebar toolbar and create new notes inside a selected folder.

### PARA method
- **Projects** — active work with a deadline
- **Areas** — ongoing responsibilities
- **Resources** — reference material like [[Literature]] or [[Science]]
- **Archives** — completed or inactive items

### Zettelkasten
One idea per note, with explicit [[Wikilinks]] between them. Structure emerges from the links rather than from a folder hierarchy. The [[Graph View]] is especially powerful with this approach because relationships become visible clusters.

## Linking strategies

1. **Hub notes** — create an index note that links to related topics (this vault's [[Welcome to Draglass]] is a hub).
2. **Daily notes** — time-stamped entry points. See [[Daily Notes]].
3. **Maps of content** — a note that outlines a large topic and links to the pieces.
4. **Progressive summarization** — start from highlights, add your own notes over time.

## Naming conventions

Descriptive titles are easier to link to:
- "Ancient Greece" rather than "AG"
- Consistent capitalization
- Full phrases over abbreviations

Since wikilinks match case-insensitively, consistency is more about readability than necessity.

## Pane layout

Organize your workspace along with your notes:

- **Left sidebar**: toggle between the file tree and [[Searching Your Vault|Global Search]] using the tabs at the top, or hide the whole sidebar with **Cmd/Ctrl + B**.
- **Right sidebar**: shows outgoing links, [[Backlinks]], and [[Tasks and Checklists|tasks]]. Toggle with **Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + B**.
- **Both sidebars**: toggle together with **Cmd/Ctrl + Alt + B** for a distraction-free writing mode.
- **Resize**: drag the dividers to widen or narrow either sidebar.

Pane sizes and open/closed state are saved automatically.
