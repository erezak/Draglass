# Political Theory

Political Theory is the study of politics, government, justice, liberty, and the organization of society.

## Core Questions

- What makes government legitimate?
- What rights do individuals have?
- What is justice?
- How should power be distributed?
- What is the good society?

These questions connect to [[Philosophy]] and ethics.

## Major Traditions

### Classical
- [[Ancient Greece]] - Plato's Republic, Aristotle's Politics
- Roman contributions - Republican ideals, law
- Virtue ethics and the good life

### Liberal
- [[Enlightenment]] roots - Locke, Rousseau, Mill
- Individual rights and freedoms
- Limited government
- Rule of law
- Market economy

### Socialist
- Critique of capitalism
- Economic equality
- Collective ownership
- Evolved in response to Industrial Revolution

### Conservative
- Tradition and stability
- Gradual change
- Limited government (in some forms)
- Social order

## Key Concepts

### Sovereignty
- Who has ultimate authority?
- People, monarch, or state?

### Rights
- Natural vs. conventional rights
- Negative vs. positive rights
- Universal vs. cultural rights

### Justice
- Distributive justice
- Procedural justice
- Retributive justice
- Connections to ethics and [[Philosophy]]

### Power
- Sources of political power
- Checks and balances
- Separation of powers
