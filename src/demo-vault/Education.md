# Education

Education is the process of facilitating learning, acquiring knowledge, skills, values, and habits.

## Forms of Education

### Formal Education
- Schools and universities
- Structured curricula
- Credentials and degrees
- Standardized assessment

### Informal Learning
- Self-directed study
- Life experience
- Mentorship
- Online resources

### Personal Knowledge Management
Tools like [[Welcome to Draglass|Draglass]] support learning. The built-in demo vault (open it from the Command Palette anytime) shows these features in action:
- [[Daily Notes]] for learning journals
- [[Wikilinks]] to connect concepts
- [[Organization Strategies]] for knowledge
- [[Backlinks]] to review connections
- [[Tasks and Checklists]] to track study goals

## Self-Directed Learning

Taking charge of your education:
1. **Set goals** — what do you want to learn?
2. **Find resources** — books, courses, [[Literature]]
3. **Create a system** — [[Workflow]] and [[Organization Strategies]]
4. **Practice** — apply knowledge to projects
5. **Review** — use [[Daily Notes]] for reflection
6. **Connect** — build knowledge with [[Wikilinks]]

> [!tip] Use the task scanner
> Write `- [ ]` items in your study notes and Draglass collects them in the [[Tasks and Checklists|Tasks panel]]. It is a simple way to keep track of what you still need to read or practice.
