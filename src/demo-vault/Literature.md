# Literature

Literature encompasses written works, especially those considered to have artistic or intellectual value.

## Forms of Literature

### Fiction
- Novels and novellas
- Short stories
- Poetry
- Drama and plays

### Non-Fiction
- Essays and articles
- Biography and autobiography
- Historical accounts (see [[History]])
- [[Philosophy]] texts
- Scientific writing (see [[Science]])

## Literary Analysis

Understanding literature involves:
- Theme and symbolism
- Character development
- Plot structure
- Historical context
- Cultural significance

## Literature and Other Disciplines

Literature intersects with many fields:

### Philosophy
Many works explore philosophical themes:
- Existentialism in fiction
- Moral dilemmas in drama
- Epistemology in detective fiction
- Ethics through narrative

### History
Literature reflects and shapes [[History]]:
- Historical novels
- Contemporary accounts
- Cultural documentation
- Social criticism

### Science
[[Science]] appears in literature:
- Science fiction exploring [[Technology]]
- Nature writing
- Medical humanities
- Popular science writing

## Cultural Impact

Great literature influences:
- Culture broadly
- [[Political Theory]] through social novels
- [[Education]] curricula
- Language evolution

## Notable Genres

- **Epic**: Homer's works, connection to [[Ancient Greece]]
- **Tragedy**: Shakespeare, exploring human nature
- **Modernism**: Joyce, Woolf, experimental forms
- **Postmodernism**: Meta-fictional approaches
